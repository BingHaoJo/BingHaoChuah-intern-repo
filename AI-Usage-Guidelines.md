What AI tools are typically used for your role?
- Copilot for the AI programming assistance and real-time autocomplete suggestions, Claude for complex reasoning and generating technical documentation, ChatGPT for brainstorming and quick debugging.

What are the benefits and risks of using AI in a professional setting?
- Using AI in your work can greatly increase your productivity and efficiency. Some tasks can be automated and save time and reduce costs. On the flip side, there is a risk of data being exposed to public AI tools. AI also generates imperfect code or information so take it as fact without checking could be dangerous.

What types of information should never be entered into AI tools?
- Personal information, confidential data, confidential code or corporate business plans.

How can you fact-check and validate AI-generated content to ensure accuracy?
- You can cross reference the facts with multiple AI tools or sources. Another way is prompting the AI to state its sources. Some AI like ChatGPT can make facts up and have no source to back it up.

Reflection

When should you use AI for assistance, and when should you rely on your own skills?
- You can use AI to help debug some problems or bugs. Our skills come in when we need to make sure that the code and facts generated by the AI are valid and compatible with your system. If we just copy and paste a code from AI's suggestion without thoroughly checking, we might break the system even more.

How can you avoid over-reliance on AI while still benefiting from it?
- By using other ways to get information namely google searching. Google searching is different from AI suggestions because you are discovering the answers yourself as opposed to having the answers told to you. The balance of using your own skills, finding the answers on your own and asking AI complex questions that is hard to answer is needed from a modern programmer.

What steps will you take to ensure data privacy when using AI tools?
- Assume every input may be public because conversations may be recorded. Use anonymous names, numbers, or identifiers when using chatbots. Use an enterprise tier if possible which guarantees data privacy from AI provider.
