Research & Learn

What are examples of professional vs. unprofessional behaviour in a casual work environment? (Hint: Think about communication, meeting etiquette, and teamwork.)
- Being punctual to meetings.
    - Professional: Be early or on time for the meeting.
    - Unprofessional: Be late for the meeting.
- Handling tasks
    - Professional: Complete tasks fully and on time.
    - Unprofessional: Did not complete the tasks at all or late.
- Respectful messages
    - Professional: Send messages in a gentle tone and use emojis sparingly if they help tone.
    - Unprofessional: Send messages with passive aggressive tones with cursed words or harrassing coworker with discrimination, cyberbullying or serious threats.

What does respectful communication look like in remote teams?
- Avoid sending constant, urgent pings outside of work hours.
- Add details and context with friendly language so tone is not misconstrued
- Always respond to the messages to let the sender know that the message is received. Send "Noted" if there aren't any replies needed.

How can you give and receive feedback professionally?
- Assume good intention and try to understand and reflect.
- Ask for clarification instead of being defensive.
- Use a friendlier tone when responding.


Reflection

Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?
- There was once when I was doing my internship in Malaysia. I witnessed two coworkers were discussing about a bug in the system. They were each giving solutions to the problem and when the solutions don't work, they continued thinking of different solutions instead of getting mad at each other. I think what made the teamwork effective is that both of them are willing to give each other the benefit of the doubt and respectfully trust each other's judgement to give the solutions a try, even if it would end in failure.

What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?
- Start the message with a friendly tone like "Hi" and use friendly language to deliver the message.
- Use emojis to help convey the tone but not always required. Respectful wording matters more than emojis.
- Avoid using discriminatory words like "r*tard" or "stupid".

How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?
- Treat each other with respect. Include coworkers in activity or conversation regardless of neurodivergence. Avoid breaching anyone's boundaries.

How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?
- Plan an agenda before the meeting. Make sure to enter the meeting with deep and thoughtful questions. Make sure you have read the project brief to fully understand the topic of the meeting.

What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?
- Take initiative to ask questions to the teammates or supervisor if stuck.
- Give regular updates in meetings.
- Take initiative to jump right in to give updates in the meeting if no one does.
- Take notes in meetings.

How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it further if you don't get a response and when would you escalate?
- I will first email them if it is not urgent, use Discord messages if it's slightly urgent and calling them for true emergencies like critical app outages affecting users, security leaks, or immediate health and safety concerns.
- If I haven't received a response on my email and it is still not urgent, I will follow up by messaging them personally on Discord.
- If it is an emergency and my supervisor has not answered any of my calls, I will escalate by calling another supervisor or the CEO for help.

- Routine requests: I will first send an email or a Discord message. If I haven't received a response after 24 hours, I will send a polite second follow-up asking for an update.
- Urgent requests: If it's urgent, I will follow up via Discord or phone within 2 to 4 hours.
- When to escalate: If a lack of response is blocking critical work from moving forward or putting a major deadline at risk, I will escalate to another supervisor or team lead with context on what was already sent.
- True emergencies: For urgent crises-such as a critical app outage, a security leak, or a health and safety issue-I will immediately call my supervisor. If they do not answer within 15-30 minutes, I will call the secondary lead or department head right away.


What are some things you won't do?
- I won't wait around for instructions.
- Not taking notes in meetings.
- Coming to meetings unprepared.
- Asking questions that could be answered using Google.
- Ignoring feedback or action items.
- Not be disrespectful to my coworkers.
