Research & Learn

What are examples of professional vs. unprofessional behaviour in a casual work environment? (Hint: Think about communication, meeting etiquette, and teamwork.)
- Being punctual to meetings.
    - Professional: Be early or on time for the meeting.
    - Unprofessional: Be late for the meeting.
- Handling tasks
    - Professional: Complete tasks fully and on time.
    - Unprofessional: Did not complete the tasks at all or late.
- Respectful messages
    - Professional: Send messages in a gentle tone with emojis.
    - Unprofessional: Send messages with passive aggressive tones with cursed words.

What does respectful communication look like in remote teams? (e.g., tone in messages, handling disagreements, responding to feedback)
- Avoid sending constant, urgent pings outside of work hours.
- Add details and context with friendly language so tone is not misconstrued
- Always respond to the messages to let the sender know that the message is received. Send "Noted" if there aren't any replies needed.

How can you give and receive feedback professionally? (e.g., focus on actions, not people; assume good intent; ask for clarification instead of reacting defensively)
- Assume good intention and try to understand and reflect.
- Ask for clarification instead of being defensive.
- Use a friendlier tone when responding.


Reflection

Have you ever experienced or witnessed great teamwork and professionalism? What made it effective?
- There was once when I was doing my internship in Malaysia. I witnessed two co-workers were discussing about a bug in the system. They were each giving solutions to the problem and when the solutions don't work, they continued thinking of different solutions instead of getting mad at each other. I think what made the teamwork effective is that both of them are willing to give each other the benefit of the doubt and respectfully trust each others judgement to give the solutions a try, even if it would end in failure.

What steps can you take to make sure your communication is clear, respectful, and inclusive?
- Start the message with a friendly tone like "Hi" and use friendly language to deliver the message. Use emojis to help convey the tone but not always needed. Avoid using discriminatory words like "r*tard" or "stupid".

How can you help create a positive, fun, and professional work environment?
- Treat each other with respect. Include co-workers in activity or conversation regardless of neurodivergence. Avoid breaching anyone's boundaries.

How can you prepare effectively for meetings with supervisors and colleagues?
- Plan an agenda before the meeting. Make sure to enter the meeting with deep and thoughtful questions. Make sure you have read the project brief to fully understand the topic of the meeting.

What would it look like to be proactive during your internship?
- Take initiative to ask questions to the teammates or supervisor if stuck.
- Give regular updates in meetings.
- Take initiative to jump right into to give updates in the meeting if no one does.
- Take notes in meetings.

How might you follow up with colleagues or supervisors if you need information/action from them? How would you escalate it further if you don't get a response and when would you escalate?
- I will first email them first if it is not urgent, use Discord messages if it's slightly urgent and calling for true emergencies.
- If I haven't got a response on my email and it is still not urgent, I will just message them on Discord.
- If it is an emergency and my supervisor has not answered any of my calls, I will call the other supervisors or the CEO for help.

What are some things you won't do?
- Don't wait around for instructions.
- Not taking notes in meetings.
- Coming to meetings unprepared.
- Asking questions that could be answered using Google.
- Ignoring feedback or action items.
- Not be disrespectful to my co-coworkers.
