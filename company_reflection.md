Company Reflection

Mission: Help people with ADHD to increase their focus and productivity in their work.

Vision: To help people with diverse types of neurodivergent disabilities to live their life easier.

Key features of Focus Bear:
- Blocking users from distractions i.e. YouTube, TikTok, Instagram
- Builds routines and habits that helps the user
- Allow the users to take breaks in between to freshen up

Why do you think Focus Bear was created?
- There are many apps out there that does not help or work for their intended users like people with ADHD. Most likely because they aren't made by people with the disabilities. Focus Bear is made for ADHD by ADHD. We need a developer that understand what the user feels to accurate provide the help that they need.

What problem is Focus Bear solving?
In this digital world full of distractions, people are more distracted than ever. With short form content, AI chatbot and generative AI existing, people are less patient, much less focused on their work when they don't consume dopamine or instant gratification. This is more prevalent with ADHD people.

Why do you think this mission is important?
A lot of people struggles to live their life with ADHD symptoms and for worse, they are countless that goes to a dark place and attempt to do things that they will regret. This mission is very important because this app has the potential to change people's lives and alleviate the pain of those who suffers them because of these disabilities.

How does Focus Bear’s work align with your personal values or interests?
One of my goals is to make games for people with physical disabilities like blindness or deafness. Therefore, creating an app that helps people is very much aligns with my value. Plus, I want to be a developer so this work also aligns with my interest.

Do you personally relate to any of the challenges that Focus Bear aims to solve?
I am not diagnosed with ADHD but because of short form content, I think I have grown to be more ADHD now so I do relate to the challenges a tiny bit.

Do you know someone with ADHD or autism or even someone without a diagnosis who has struggled socially or to perform well at uni? How might Focus Bear help them?
I do know someone in my university project who gets distracted from work so much that he didn't finish his tasks which drags our team down. This app might help him to refocus himself to the work as opposed to not finishing it.
