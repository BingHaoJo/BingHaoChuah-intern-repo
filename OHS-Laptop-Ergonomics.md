Research & Learn

What are the risks of using a laptop without an external monitor or keyboard?
- Using laptop keyboard forces the wrist into unnatural angles which could result in Carpal Tunnel Syndrome.
- Because a laptop screen sits much lower than eye level, you must constantly look downward which could lead to chronic neck, shoulder, and upper back pain.

What ergonomic equipment can improve posture when working on a laptop?
- An external monitor that is bigger than the laptop screen
- Laptop stands
- External keyboards
- Ergonomic chairs

What adjustments should be made to monitor height, chair position, and desk setup for a healthier workspace?
- The monitor height should be at eye level and at one arm's length away from your face.
- Adjust your chair so your feet rest flat on the floor and your thighs are parallel to the ground. Position the chair's lumbar support so it fits snugly into the natural curve of your lower back.
- The surface of your desk should align perfectly with your forearms when your elbows are bent at 90 degrees. Your wrists should remain flat and straight, not bent up or down while typing.

What are some daily habits that reduce the impact of prolonged laptop use?
- Use the micro breaks from Focus Bear app.
- Strect your body after 30 minutes of sitting down.
- Let your eyes rest after 30 minutes of looking at the screen.
