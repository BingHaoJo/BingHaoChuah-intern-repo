🔍 Research & Learn
What are common cyber security threats in a remote work environment?
- Phishing, credential theft, malware, ransomware, physical theft.

What are best practices for keeping your devices and accounts secure?
- Setup muilt-factor Authentication, have unique password for every account, get a trusted password manager, get a trusted antivirus, keep devices up to date

Why is it important to lock your computer when away from your desk?
- Leaving your computer unlocked and unattended in a public spaces allows strangers to get access to your local data and confidential information.

How should you handle phishing attempts and suspicious links?
- It is important to not click on the link and ignore the email or message. Verify the source independently by doing your research or asking others. We should also report the email or message to make sure the email or message provider can handle it.

What makes a strong password, and why should you use a password manager?
- A strong and secure password is long, random with different letters, symbols and number and does not have any related information to the user. A password manager is very useful because you can save a lot of password from all your accounts so you don't have to remember all of them and all you need to remember is one master password for the password manager.

Why is two-factor authentication (2FA) important, and when should you enable it?
- Two-factor authentication is important because it stops strangers from logging into your account when they found out your login credentials. It is like needing multiple keys to open a door. Strangers won't be able to enter if they only stole one key.
